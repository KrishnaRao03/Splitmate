What Splitmate Stores

Splitmate stores your account details, groups, expenses, payments, notes, tasks, device notification tokens, rewards balance, and settings needed to run the app.

Payments

Splitmate never collects or holds money. Members pay each other directly with services such as Interac e-Transfer, PayPal or UPI; Splitmate stores the payment details you choose to share (your Interac email, PayPal.Me username or UPI ID) and a record of each payment, which the person paid confirms.

Grocery Prices

Grocery comparison may use retailer or grocery price APIs. Notes are used only to identify grocery-list items when price comparison is requested.

Ads and Analytics

Splitmate may show ads in the free app and may use app analytics to understand performance, crashes, and usage patterns.

Your Data

You can export account data from Profile & Settings. Account deletion support must be completed before public app-store launch.

Use of Splitmate

Splitmate helps groups track shared expenses, tasks, notes, payments, grocery comparisons, rewards, and memberships.

Payments and Rewards

Earning Splitmate Points is paused. Points you already have are kept. Rewards, gift cards, and cash redemption may require identity, fraud, and provider checks.

Membership

Premium membership benefits depend on the active plan shown in the app. Mobile app subscriptions must be connected through approved app-store billing before public launch.

Ads

The free version may include ads. Premium plans may reduce or remove ads depending on the final plan.

Accuracy

Expense balances and grocery prices should be reviewed before payment or purchase. Retail prices can change and may vary by location.
